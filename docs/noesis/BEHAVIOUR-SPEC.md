# Noesis teaching behaviour specification

Written from observation and reading of the reference at revision 713377f, without copying its text, for clean-room use. Implementers must not consult LearnHarness. This is a behavioural target, not a claim that a language model will always follow its instructions. “Matches” means the corresponding Noesis mechanism or instruction exists; it does not establish equal teaching quality. “Differs” includes partial implementations. Parameters labelled “judgment” are not hard limits. The examples are acceptance scenarios, not quotations or prescribed lesson text.

The checked parity manifest includes only new behaviours with an existing Rust test. Other scenarios below are proposed acceptance tests and gaps, not automated coverage. Statuses reflect the Noesis source and web client at the time of writing; prompt instructions and deterministic enforcement are distinguished.

## Teaching and practice

**B01 — Question presentation.** A prediction is presented before instruction and a check after instruction; the backend identifies checks as initially collapsed and predictions as open. Parameters: one open question; prediction and check are separate roles. Test: a tagged prediction then a tagged check produce open and collapsed presentation flags respectively. **Noesis: differs** — the backend has the flags, but the current card collapses answered questions rather than hiding an unanswered check.

**B02 — Redundant reasoning.** Remove a chain when another retained route derives its conclusion from the same starting facts; retain the necessary intermediate steps. Parameters: all premises of a chain must be available. Test: given A→B, B→C, A→C and A+D→B, the latter two are removed. **Noesis: matches** for this example; see K07 for the different general selection procedure.

**T01 — Foundations and discovery.** Establish facts the learner can accept in their stated context before building on them. For each derived idea, make the need for the next move and its dependence on prior facts intelligible. Do not present a conditional claim as universally true to make it sound foundational. Parameters: one reasoning move and one new fact per teaching unit; foundations are checked before use. Test: a learner accepts a definition but not a consequence → establish the missing dependency before using the consequence. **Noesis: differs** — it asks for a minimal prerequisite plan and checked steps, but does not specify this foundation-and-derivation discipline.

**T02 — Plan and adaptation.** Use the probe and goal to build a prerequisite map from held foundations to the goal, omitting detours and already secure knowledge. Show the approach and map, end the turn and obtain agreement; revise it when later evidence changes the assumed prerequisites. A map node is a landmark, not necessarily one exchange. Parameters: prerequisite edges point toward their dependants; map size follows the subject; reference slugs also permit underscores. Test: learner exposes a missing prerequisite midway → insert it and teach it before the dependent concept. **Noesis: differs** — it supports replacement and agreement in prompts, but describes nodes as short exchanges and its slug rules differ; agreement is not a deterministic gate.

**T03 — Predict, explain, check.** Ordinarily invite an attempt at the upcoming move before revealing it; then explicitly reconcile the attempt with the explanation and check the new understanding. Select discovery by the learner when plausible, explanation when the step is beyond their reach or energy. Skip prediction for a pure repair or wrap-up. Parameters: one question per turn; no fixed explanation length. Test: prediction is incorrect → the next instruction addresses the prediction and its actual consequence before checking. **Noesis: differs** — prediction is optional where appropriate; the full cycle and resolution are not specified.

**T04 — Diagnostic checks and repair.** A check must be solvable from what has been taught, preferably in a new application; plausible alternatives distinguish different wrong models. A miss causes diagnosis and repair before advancing; use recall when application is inappropriate. Parameters: one check per reasoning unit; no fixed number of repairs. Test: an incorrect answer reveals a confused prerequisite → work on that prerequisite before the next dependent step. **Noesis: differs** — prompts require checks and repair but do not specify all of these design constraints.

**T05 — Probe.** Clarify an underspecified goal, then locate the secure and uncertain ends of each relevant prerequisite strand. Start near the highest plausible prior knowledge, move upward rapidly after confident success and downward after difficulty, and compare related strands. Inspect reasoning as well as correctness and confidence, including the reach of an apparent misconception; do not teach during measurement. Parameters: stop when the boundary is adequate for planning, not after a fixed question count. Test: confident successes above the initial guess → probe further upward until a meaningful boundary appears; return the boundary and suggested start. **Noesis: differs** — it starts near the expected middle and brackets strands, but lacks the stronger cross-strand and misconception-extent instructions.

**T06 — Confidence and reasoning.** Consider correctness, expressed confidence and both the learner's explanation of the answer and their thoughts while reading. A correct uncertain response may be a guess; a confident error can expose a stable wrong model. Treat a refusal to guess as missing knowledge, not a wrong answer. Parameters: confidence choices 0, 30, 50, 70, 90, 100; default 50; for n options, confidence at or below 1/n is at or below chance. Test: correct answer at 30 → check or reinforce before assuming security; no-answer → record no confidence or wrong verdict. **Noesis: differs** — it captures confidence and optional reasoning and derives states, but lacks the separate reading-thought channel and the chance-relative interpretation.

**T07 — Question construction.** Commit the answer and its rationale before presenting a graded question. Make alternatives comparable in form and individually diagnostic; avoid revealing the key through wording or formatting. Allow genuine preference questions without a correctness key. Parameters: choices can be shuffled; multi-select uses exact-set grading. Test: an option tied to a specific mistaken model is selected → feedback identifies the error after submission, with the key hidden until then. **Noesis: differs** — it seals and grades keys and shuffles options, but the detailed distractor craft is not instructed.

**T08 — Examples and visual work.** Choose a worked example where it makes the reasoning concrete, then use an appropriate check. For a dynamic process, an interactive instrument may let the learner vary inputs; exact geometry calls for a verified figure, while an existing learner image may be shown or accurately annotated. Parameters: artifact jobs are asynchronous; verify a demo runs and inspect a figure before delivery; do not label an unseen feature. Test: a learner supplies a circuit photo and asks about a visible connection → show it and annotate the grounded connection. **Noesis: differs** — its teacher prompt requests examples and fading support, but lacks these artifact and image workflows.

**T09 — Verification and voice.** Verify uncertain external facts before relying on them, disclose unresolved uncertainty and revisit it; correct errors openly. Use direct, precise language, plainly identify mistakes, avoid praise, performed excitement and false certainty. Parameters: independent research may run in the background; cite its sources. Test: a version-dependent claim cannot be verified → identify the uncertainty instead of asserting the claim. **Noesis: differs** — prompts request caution and graph search, but use a warmer voice and omit the research and uncertainty follow-up workflow.

**T10 — Lesson formatting.** Render lesson text and question fields as structured Markdown, with code blocks, tables and diagrams where they clarify; format mathematical expressions as math, and use prominent callouts sparingly for key ideas. Parameters: inline and display math are distinct; no fixed section count. Test: a derivation with a formula and a comparison → math renders as math and the comparison remains legible as a table. **Noesis: differs** — chat supports Markdown but these authoring rules and full reference rendering are not specified in its teaching prompts.

**T11 — Practice selection.** Exercise retrieval on material already held, using varied contexts and interleaved targets. Prefer weak, influential and less recently active targets; begin with easy recalls, then progress through recall, discrimination and composition as evidence permits. Parameters: 1–3 opening recalls; aim near 85% success per retrieval decision, not per whole problem; after two consecutive concluded misses on one target switch targets. Test: a target succeeds in recall but fails discrimination → reduce difficulty or return to recall, then revisit in a different context. **Noesis: differs** — it supports the three modes and interleaving but not this ordering, selection or pacing doctrine.

**T12 — Practice feedback and pacing.** Commit a solved problem before showing it; diagnose the first failed decision, give only the smallest useful hint and wait. Stay on the problem until solved or explicitly ended, then wait for the learner to request the next. Retest misses, heavily assisted attempts and surrendered problems on a fresh surface. Parameters: six reference hint levels: attention, pattern, candidate class, named proposition, outlined move, worked step; one increment per request. Test: a wrong intermediate choice → point to the decision without supplying the answer; after conclusion no next problem appears until requested. **Noesis: differs** — its six levels have different meanings, and its prompt permits concluding and posing the next problem in the same turn.

## Knowledge and evidence

**K01 — Propositions.** Represent subject knowledge as independently meaningful claims, with a short name, alternate phrasings and source provenance. A learner's mistake belongs in learner evidence; a corrected fact may enter the graph. Parameters: no learner mastery follows merely from graph presence. Test: two sources support the same fact → one linked proposition retains both sources. **Noesis: differs** — it has propositions and provenance, but also merges identical normalized content without the reference's separate identity decision.

**K02 — Chains and junctions.** A reasoning chain joins a set of premises to one conclusion; all premises are needed together. A single premise may render as an arrow; multiple premises require a junction so they cannot be mistaken for independent proofs. Parameters: premise order is immaterial; empty, unresolved and self-referential chains are rejected or discarded. Test: A and B jointly imply C → the graph shows one joint junction, not two independent implications. **Noesis: differs** — the backend stores a premise set, while the current graph UI flattens it into separate arrows.

**K03 — Relations.** Preserve the direction and type of a connection: prerequisite links in the plan express teaching order, while graph chains express joint support. Do not infer mastery or a new support relation from visual proximity. Parameters: one conclusion per chain. Test: a plan places A before B without a graph argument → no support chain is invented. **Noesis: matches** in its separate plan and graph models.

**K04 — Extraction and identity.** Extract standalone true claims and explicit supporting arguments from lesson material, anchored to the material that asserted them. Compare new claims with global candidates in a separate identity review; resolve endpoints before merging, retain source attribution, and mark material covered only after a successful merge. Parameters: reference batches about 4,096 estimated tokens, up to 3 concurrent extractors, sequential batches within a source. Test: two batches restate a fact → review links them, and a failed merge leaves its coverage unadvanced. **Noesis: differs** — it has extraction, audit and per-unit transactional coverage, but uses different batch sizes, bounded candidate selection and deterministic identity.

**K05 — Clusters.** Group a proposition by its first surviving source, including imported material. A multi-premise junction belongs to the majority birth group of its premises; ties use its own source. Only show a region when it contains at least 3 propositions. Test: three claims from an imported source and their joint chain → show the source region and assign the junction by the premise majority. **Noesis: differs** — snapshots group by session source only and the current UI does not draw those regions.

**K06 — Deletion.** Deleting a proposition removes chains that use it; deleting a chain removes that chain. Deleting a source offers preservation or removal of unsupported contributions, retains shared facts and chains, removes source-specific aliases when appropriate, and clears its coverage and obsolete positions. Parameters: source deletion is scoped to the selected source. Test: two sources assert one fact; remove one with contribution cleanup → the fact and the remaining source survive, but the removed source's unique alias does not. **Noesis: differs** — it supports preservation and unsupported cleanup, but cannot remove aliases by source attribution.

**K07 — Graph simplification.** Compare arguments with the same conclusion; retain a minimal premise set and remove an argument whose conclusion is already reached through retained premises and their ancestry. Parameters: reference considers smaller sets first, breaking ties by creation order and identity, against the pre-simplification graph. Test: A→B and A+D→B → retain the former; A→B, B→C and A→C → remove the shortcut. **Noesis: differs** — it simplifies against the currently retained graph in a different order, so cyclic or tied alternatives may choose differently.

**K08 — Value cleanup.** At membership comparisons only, remove surrounding whitespace and punctuation accidentally attached to an option value, role or target; preserve interior characters and all free text. Parameters: surrounding colons, periods, quote marks and backticks are removable repeatedly. Test: a quoted option identifier with a leading colon matches its clean identifier; the same punctuation in a lesson sentence remains unchanged. **Noesis: differs** — argument healing exists, but identical cleanup at every comparison and presentation seam is not established.

**K09 — Layout.** Supply stable node identities, links, source groups and saved resting positions so the graph can settle incrementally without losing the learner's spatial map. Parameters: reference settling uses 300 ticks, 0.85 anchor retention, link distance 40, charge −240, cluster pull 0.15, gravity 0.2 initially and 0.12 incrementally; depth accounts for cycles. Test: add a proposition to an existing graph → existing anchored positions remain near their previous locations and the new node settles around them. **Noesis: differs** — its canvas computes positions without the reference's persisted layout and cluster inputs.

**K10 — Conversation compaction.** Keep the full transcript for the learner and extraction, while supplying the model a summary plus a recent tail. Preserve the learner's evolving understanding, lesson trajectory, exact dependency map and current pending interaction in the summary. Parameters: default threshold 150,000 estimated tokens, capped by model window less 16,384; retain about 20,000 recent tokens; cap individual tool-result text in summary input at 2,000 characters. Test: compact a lesson with a pending question → transcript remains complete and the next model context retains the map and pending question. **Noesis: differs** — generic context compaction exists, but an equivalent Noesis-specific summary contract is not established.

**L01 — Evidence and next turn.** Feed answers, confidence, reasoning, probe observations and practice outcomes back into subsequent instructional choices; a correct guess should not erase contradictory reasoning. Parameters: reference interpretation is prompt-driven, not a verified deterministic mastery score. Test: learner answers correctly but explains a faulty model → next instruction investigates the faulty model rather than assuming mastery. **Noesis: differs** — it also derives persistent per-concept states: initially 2-day half-life, clamped 1–180 days; two successful days are needed for demonstrated status, and confidence and hint thresholds affect classification. These extra computations are not reference parity.

## Client contract

**U01 — Chat and interaction.** Preserve ordered text and structured question/problem events, including streaming, stop, retry, queued steering and drafts. Gate the ordinary composer when an interaction owns input; reveal the committed answer only after submission. Parameters: prediction opens immediately, a teaching check waits behind a reveal; confidence defaults to 50. Test: a pending check → lesson text is readable first, answer stays hidden, and the check opens on request. **Noesis: differs** — it waits for a committed reply instead of teaching streaming, and its current question card presents the unanswered check directly.

**U02 — Graph view.** Provide directed joint-support geometry, groups, labels, provenance and stable positions for inspection, search and navigation. Filtering must not leave dangling links or a selected item hidden by the filter. Parameters: camera and resting positions persist per device; group display threshold is 3 propositions. Test: search excludes the selected proposition → hide its incident edges and clear its selection. **Noesis: differs** — it supports search, details, provenance and deletion, but omits reference junctions, cluster regions and persisted graph layout.

## Gaps ranked by likely impact on lesson quality

1. Foundation-first motivated derivation, per-move diagnostic checks, and adaptive repair (T01–T04).
2. The richer probe and reasoning/confidence interpretation, including distinct thinking channels (T05–T07, L01).
3. Practice progression, correct hint semantics, retests and learner-controlled pacing (T11–T12).
4. Correct joint-chain visualization and source-aware extraction/identity (K01–K05, U02).
5. Verified visual aids, research workflow and lesson rendering (T08–T10, U01).
6. Source-specific deletion, stable layout, value cleanup and teaching-aware compaction (K06–K10).
