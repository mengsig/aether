Before this pass, the web app had one shared page kit but still read as a kit: boxes inside boxes, bordered buttons and chips, a serif display face over a sans text face, a dark gradient rail, uppercase labels, and empty states with a glyph. After it, one type family carries the hierarchy, lists are parted by hairlines, buttons and chips are flat tints, the rail shares the page's ground, and every motion runs on four duration tokens that reduced motion zeroes. Every "Before" image is the frontend at 1db2f324 (the branch's base); every "After" image is this branch. Both sets were taken headless on the end-to-end stack (real daemons, a faux engine, temporary roots), so no real credentials or operator data appear.

Design principles

Type

One family, the system sans (SF Pro, Segoe UI Variable, Roboto, Noto Sans in that order), for display and text alike. Display text is the same face set larger, bolder and tighter. Mono is for ids, code and keys only. Numerals are tabular everywhere, so counts and times line up.

Weights 400, 500 and 600. Display tracking is -0.02 em at 24 px and up, -0.01 em at 16 to 20 px. Labels are sentence case; no uppercase, no letter-spaced small caps.

Spacing

A 4 px grid, --space-1 to --space-12. The page gap is 24, a card's padding is 20, the main column's padding is 32 on the sides and 16 above, rows in a list sit 12 to 16 apart, and controls are 36 px tall (28 px for the small size). Radii 6, 8, 12 and 16 px: fields and chips 6 or 8, cards 12, dialogs 16.

Palette

Neutral ground, white surfaces, one green that does the pointing, blue only for the focus ring. Every text colour reads at or above 4.5:1 on every surface in both themes, checked by script.

Structure

A card is the one box a view gets; inside it, items are parted by hairlines, never boxed again. Buttons, chips, badges and pills are flat tints with no border; only fields and cards have one. Each view has one filled primary action; everything else is a tint or plain text. The navigation rail shares the page's ground and is parted from it by a hairline; the current link is a lifted white row, not a coloured bar.

Motion

Four durations and three eases, nothing else: --motion-instant 60 ms, --motion-fast 120 ms, --motion-base 200 ms, --motion-slow 280 ms. Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce all four are 0 ms, so every animation and transition below stops; web/e2e/specs/smoke/motion.spec.ts walks the DOM with that media emulated and asserts no element keeps a duration.

  • Agent names are plain medium-weight text that turns green on hover instead of underlined links.
  • Column heads are sentence case in the faint label style.

Pairing

  • The eyebrow is the kit's quiet label, not an uppercase accent line; the pairing phrase is the one display moment at 32 px.
  • The form rises in on the base token and the primary button is the only filled control on the screen.

Queue

  • Filter chips are flat tints; the pressed chip inverts to ink on paper. Counts are faint and tabular.
  • The status summary breathes more (24 px gaps) and the paused state reads at 16 px bold.
  • Question rows carry a left rule in the operator colour and rise in on the fast token.

Inbox

History

Topology

Agent

  • Fact labels are sentence case; the menu sits at the end of the title row.
  • Messages are hairline-separated: yours on a white surface, the agent's plain on the page, at a 72-character measure.

Controls

  • Sections are kit cards whose items are hairline rows, not cards inside cards.
  • Standing notes are a left info rule on the second surface; text areas and selects use the standard field border with a hover state.
  • The observer view keeps every control visible with its refusal reason, all in the new flat button style.

Memory

  • Store labels are sentence case; the big numbers are bold 20 px display, not mono.
  • Tool rows and turns are hairline rows; an untraced turn is dimmed rather than boxed.
  • The screen's own reduced-motion override is gone: the tokens already handle it.

Activity

Item

Settings
