Spacing and sizes

A 4 px grid; controls come in two heights (36 px, 28 px small) with one emphasised curve; cards and dialogs keep the 12 and 16 px radii. Pickers, combos, switches, segmented controls, tabs, dialogs and toasts share these sizes, so a form reads as one system.

Motion

The four duration tokens (60, 120, 200, 280 ms) and three eases carry every transition: pages, sheets and dialogs rise or pop in, pickers open on the fast token, graph layouts and cameras interpolate on the base token, a running tool's glyph pulses. Under prefers-reduced-motion: reduce every duration is 0 ms, which smoke/motion.spec.ts asserts by walking the DOM.
